# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Believers seeking biblical teaching: some new to faith, some mature and hungry to go deeper. They arrive from social media (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok) or search, mostly on phones, in quiet personal moments: morning devotions, a commute, late evenings. Their job: find teaching that feeds them (a sermon, an article, a structured course) and, once they trust the ministry, stay connected (newsletter) or support it (giving).

Secondary: donors and monthly partners; the two founders (Brett and Jude) administering content through Sanity Studio and the admin portal. The admin portal is a product-register surface and stays utilitarian.

## Product Purpose

Fire Within University is the teaching home of The Fire Within LLC, a Christian ministry. It offers free sermons, articles, multi-part teaching series, and structured Bible courses, funded by donations. Success looks like: people consuming teaching and returning for more, subscribing to the newsletter, and giving to sustain the work. Everything is free; generosity is the response, never the price.

## Brand Personality

Reverent, warm, alive. Sacred but inviting: the fire is a living warmth (candlelight, embers, a glowing hearth), never hype-flame or revival-stadium energy. The visual register is premium-cinematic darkness; the homepage hero (light breaking through a stone doorway) is the brand's defining image. The voice is direct, scriptural, and personal, like a trusted teacher rather than an institution.

## Anti-references

- Dated church websites: clip-art doves, rainbow gradients, stocky template layouts, 2010 web energy. This is the single thing the site must never resemble.
- By extension: generic SaaS landing patterns (floating cards, pastel gradients, marketing-speak). The site should never feel like a tech product.

## Design Principles

1. **Light against darkness.** The core visual metaphor. Warmth and glow emerge from deep brown darkness, like candlelight in a stone room. Every page carries an ember of the homepage's fire; no page is a flat dark slab.
2. **Scripture is honored content.** Verses get a distinct, set-apart treatment (serif, italic, attributed, linked). Scripture is never decoration or filler.
3. **Reverence over hype.** Motion is slow, warm, and physical: rising, glowing, breathing. Never bouncy, elastic, or attention-grabbing. Calm confidence, not urgency.
4. **The content is the gift.** No paywalls, no dark patterns, no aggressive conversion pressure. Giving and subscribing are invitations extended with dignity.
5. **Craft as witness.** Execution quality reflects care for the mission. Details are worth sweating because the work is an offering, not just a website.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. The dark theme maintains verified text contrast (4.5:1 or better; earlier audit fixes raised placeholder and gold-text contrast). `prefers-reduced-motion` is fully respected, including replacing the autoplaying hero video with a still image. Full keyboard navigability (focus traps in modals, visible focus rings), 44px touch targets, screen-reader labeling per the completed a11y audit.
